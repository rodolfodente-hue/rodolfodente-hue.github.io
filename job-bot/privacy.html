Job-Bot privacy policy

Job-Bot is a private tool used only by its owner.

  • Who uses it: only its owner, through one Google account created for this tool.
  • What it reaches: only the Google Drive files and spreadsheets shared with that account.
  • What it does with them: reads and updates them for the owner. The content is processed by the AI service that runs the tool, so that it can do this work.
  • What it does not do: it does not sell this data, use it for advertising, or collect data from anyone who visits this page.
