PC Guardian privacy policy

PC Guardian is a private tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.

  • Who uses it: only its owner, with the owner's own Google account.
  • What it reaches: read-only access to the owner's Gmail. It searches only for breach notices from the Have I Been Pwned service and opens only those messages. It cannot send, change or delete email.
  • What it does with them: it reads the breach name and the affected address from each notice and sends the owner an alert on the owner's own channels. The rest of the message is not kept. The access key stays encrypted on the owner's computer.
  • What it does not do: it does not share or sell this data, use it for advertising or for training AI models, or collect data from anyone who visits this page.

PC Guardian's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
